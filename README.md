# 🌍 Salary Converter — Compare Salaries Across 2,483 Neighborhoods

**[Try it free → salary-converter.com](https://salary-converter.com)**

A free tool that compares salaries across **2,483 neighborhoods** in **113 cities worldwide** — factoring in taxes, cost of living, and exchange rates.

## The Problem

Every cost-of-living calculator treats entire cities as one number. But "NYC" could mean Tribeca ($4,500/month rent) or Inwood ($1,800/month). "London" could mean Chelsea or Tottenham. These aren't the same financial reality.

Salary Converter solves this by comparing at the **neighborhood level**.

## Features

- **Neighborhood-level comparisons** — 2,483 neighborhoods across 113 cities
- **Tax calculations** — using OECD data (federal, state/provincial, local)
- **Cost of living** — from Numbeo and Expatistan, weighted by neighborhood
- **Exchange rates** — ECB data for international comparisons
- **Single & Family mode** — family mode factors in childcare costs
- **Retirement/Savings tool** — how long will your savings last in each city?
  - Visa requirements
  - Tax obligations
  - Quality of life scores

## Data Sources

| Data | Source |
|------|--------|
| Cost of Living | [Numbeo](https://www.numbeo.com) + [Expatistan](https://www.expatistan.com) |
| Tax Rates | [OECD](https://www.oecd.org) |
| Exchange Rates | [ECB](https://www.ecb.europa.eu) |

All comparisons use a **NYC baseline (COLI = 100)** for consistency.

## Use Cases

- **Job offer comparison** — Is $150K in Austin better than $190K in NYC?
- **Relocation planning** — Will my salary go further if I move abroad?
- **Remote work negotiation** — Push back on geo-based pay adjustments with real data
- **Retirement planning** — Where will my savings stretch the furthest?
- **Expat financial planning** — Compare purchasing power across countries
